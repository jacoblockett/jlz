# JLZ complementary-placement coverage — verified versus not yet tested

**Status as of 2026-10-20:** current accepted native Go codec with **layered BCJ→native BWT plus built-in Go PGO** on `main`, archive **58,767,739 B / 4.7873709325 s encode / 1.8721679705 s decode**; no pending `candidate`. This matrix corrects the common research error **“standalone replacement failed, therefore the mathematical idea failed.”** It is an **audit of actual tested placements**, not an assertion that every possible combination has been tried. Read alongside [measured placement report](../reports/2026-10-20-layered-architecture-reassessment.md), [historical complementary audit](complementary-integration-audit.md), [strategy ledger](strategy-ledger.md), [attempted theoretical ideas](attempted-ideas.md).

## Interpretation and hard rules

- **CURRENT** — already a working native JLZ stage, additive with the accepted overall compressor.
- **HISTORIC-LAYER** — a reversible selected-substream or additive-mode experiment actually performed historically, with the experimental stage and original data specified. May have used an earlier JLZ baseline; **not proof it wins with current**.
- **NEW-SCREEN** — actually executed on selected original full-Silesia source blocks with complete current *component* payload/framing and verified inverse; not a full archive result.
- **NEW-INTEGRATED** — actual full current-JLZ encode/decode of all twelve original files; **10-run benchmark** is distinguished from one-pass screen.
- **OPEN** — this particular placement/implementation has not been tested against current and is **NOT a failure**. Needs a credible size-and-speed path before work.
- **CLOSED-PLACEMENT** — a concrete implementation and placement was disproved economically or structurally by real tests; **not a theorem disproval**.
- **DEFER** — insufficient decoder-cost, format independence, or plausible CPU budget; not a benchmark result.

**All accepted production promotions require nonregression in complete Silesia compressed bytes, ten-run median compression time and ten-run median decompression time.** Even a huge ratio win is not enough to justify slower encoding or decoding without explicit user authorization. Synthetic mathematical controls, historical transformed-data+Zstd/DEFLATE results, and unpaired component timings must not be represented as integrated standalone JLZ wins.

## What the new results *actually* establish

The newly integrated BCJ→BWT option is the strongest observed layered current-JLZ candidate in this reassessment: **fully charged 60,244,257 B**, with ten actual complete Silesia runs and exact decoding. It improves **compressed size and decompression** while slowing **compression median** nominally **1.1668%** versus retained approved reference; **REJECT**. Adjacent file-level distributed preflight gating produced the same bytes in one complete screen but was also slower, so do not tune density or gate thresholds.

The three other newly tested *substream* transform families (LZP→BWT/literals; GF(2) transpose/3-lag on math literals; BCJ→native/predictive) mostly expanded native packed bytes. A short grammar on native lengths gained a little but is not viable as an extra full mode under strict CPU requirements. Combining two previously size-positive but CPU-regressing algorithms (BCJ + priced math parser) was **worse**, empirically, not hypothetically.

## Rules for future experiments and status corrections

1. **Before rejecting a scientific strategy**, name the exact previously tested source domain and placement; compare its potential uses in original input, match commands, literal/residual streams, BWT symbols, additive coordinates, model metadata and group-reference state.
2. **Layered integration is the default**, not optional. An isolated reversible prototype verifies feasibility but does not prove performance against JLZ. Conversely, a demonstrably unfavorable **actual layered component** can justify stopping a concrete variant before burning ten complete benchmarks.
3. **A theory can remain OPEN even if a placement is CLOSED.** Do not mark unimplemented full Whitehead/Tietze/syzygy, biological motifs, latent-state models, or hypergraph theories as measured failures because simpler analogues failed.
4. **Cost both axes**: compressed payload including model/selector/inverse metadata, and encoder+decoder CPU plus memory/format compatibility. Do not turn combinatorial “try all pairs of failed experiments” into a slow research spiral; prioritize materially distinct mechanisms that could satisfy the strict three-metric Pareto gate.
5. **Only new passing integrated candidates can change production.** None qualified in this audit. Do not promise work in the background or keep a candidate on main while unaccepted. Preserve research evidence but not rejected source.

All original trials were implemented in disposable `/mnt/data/jlz_layer_*` Go worktrees and have no production Go commits. The source-size/inverse screens are *not ten-run benchmarks*, except the explicitly labeled BCJ→BWT full-codec candidate.
