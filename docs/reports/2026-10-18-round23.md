# Round 23: Broader native BWT selection and provably safe sample rejection (accepted)

**Date:** 2026-10-18. **Decision:** ACCEPT. This research began from exact source files checked against the canonical GitHub `main` blob SHAs; two source edits were subsequently promoted directly to `main`, without any research branch or PR.

## Hypothesis and implementation

Round 21's original-input BWT + MTF + zero-run + Huffman representation was selected only after two preflight conditions that favored exceptionally high mathematical-region compressibility. That inadvertently suppressed profitable BWT competition on some files. The sample gate also wasted suffix sorts after the decision had become mathematically impossible.

1. In `tryEarlyRegional` (`region_first.go`), retain the three original representative 256 KiB mathematical/native probes and their requirement that each mathematical sample beat the native sample by approximately 10%. Loosen the redundant aggregate mathematical/native threshold from 75% to **80%**, and the aggregate mathematical/raw threshold from 50% to **80%**. The *actual serialized* BWT samples must still win in at least two positions by at least 5% in total before taking the new block-sorting path.
2. In `sampleBWTPreference` (`bwt_native.go`), compute the complete mathematical sample baseline before BWT probes. Stop producing BWT samples if `wins + remaining < 2` or `100 * encodedSoFar > 95 * fullMathBaseline`. Nonnegative remaining BWT payload sizes cannot restore an impossible vote count or total margin. This affects only encoder decision work, not decoder representation or the winning BWT blocks.

No new mode, setting, external backend, file format or suffix-array serialization dependency. The existing BWT block type remains 12. The experiment is distinct from the previous Round 17–20 rejected entropy-context microtweaks. The newly supplied `COMPRESSION_ATTEMPTED_IDEAS_INVENTORY.md` is the already canonical historical attempted-ideas inventory, not a new record of untried theories; this change is a measured follow-up to Round 21's structural representation.

## Corpus and reproducibility warning

The complete user-provided `silesia.zip` was extracted into the test environment: **12 source files, 211,938,580 input bytes**. Source SHA-256 values are stored in [Round 23 corpus manifest](2026-10-18-round23-silesia-sha256.txt), and archives were not added to the repository. Toolchain: **Go 1.23.2 linux/amd64**. Independent local baseline and candidate source trees, with the exact same data and environment, were alternately executed in ten paired whole-corpus in-memory encoder/decoder runs. Compression and decoding clocks exclude disk reading and test setup; archive sizes include all headers, framing and SHA-256. Each paired run verified all 12 reconstructed files byte-exactly.

**Important:** Rebuilding the same `main` Round 21 source on this supplied corpus with Go 1.23.2 produces **64,505,708 B**, not the historically approved Round 21 **63,770,766 B**. This discrepancy is real and reproduced in repeated local runs; its cause was **not established** (input-byte identity against the original historical research corpus and toolchain differences cannot presently be ruled out). Accordingly, no local run is represented as a reproduction of the prior historical figure, and no speedup is claimed relative to the older host's timings. All percentage changes below are paired against the untouched local Round 21 source and current input hashes.

## Verification and compatibility

- `go test -count=1 ./...` and `go vet ./...` passed for candidate.
- Targeted `go test -race -count=1` passed on native BWT roundtrips, cross-group references, and parallel-native integrity. A broader multi-pattern race attempt exceeded its wall-clock budget and is **not** claimed to pass.
- Independently rebuilt **27 baseline and 27 candidate archives** from 12 Silesia and 15 bundled fixtures. All **27/27** candidate archives roundtripped byte-exactly. The candidate decoder decoded all **27/27** baseline archives; the unchanged Round 21 decoder decoded all **27/27** candidate archives.
- **25/27 archive byte streams remain identical**; only `mr` and `x-ray` changed. Their already-existing BWT block type requires a decoder from Round 21 or newer. Nothing implies pre-Round-21 forward compatibility; those older decoders are expected to reject new BWT blocks.
- No executable binaries or research corpus files were committed. Local benchmark executables and archive outputs were disposable working copies.

## Verdict and next priority

**PROMOTE Round 23.** The paired experiment improves all three aggregate measures without archive-size regressions and preserves Round 21 decoder interoperability. Changes remain two narrow source edits.

**Next priority:** first establish Silesia input/toolchain identity for historical reproducibility, and test automatic BWT gating on independent legally reusable structured, mixed binary and textual corpora rather than overfitting the two newly selected Silesia files. The historical mathematical attempted-ideas inventory already records many negative synthetic-only and transform-plus-external-codec prototypes. A future substantial structural idea should be measured on natural source bytes as a standalone JLZ candidate, not inferred from diagnostics.
