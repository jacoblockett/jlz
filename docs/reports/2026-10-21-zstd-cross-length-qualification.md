# Zstd cross-history split-length qualification and related batched refinements

**Date:** 2026-10-21. **Disposition:** strong byte-exact compression improvement in **TEN complete 12-original-Silesia candidate-only runs**, but inconclusive comparative speed and new wire subtype. **KEEP AS CANDIDATE, NO PRODUCTION PROMOTION.** Previous source/main and committed root PGO unchanged.

## Mechanism and novelty

Investigated the last session's Meta Zstd `zstd_compress_sequences.c` and `zstd_compress_superblock.c` inspired metadata coding. JLZ **already** separated literal and match lengths in local math modes since historical Round 16. The newly screened distinct placement is independently decoded **cross-history block type9**: after the existing single native exact/XOR/ADD tokenization, calculate actual incumbent packed merged-length stream bytes and separately packed literal/reference length bytes; select a new **type20** only when its two complete stream frames are smaller. The decoder reuses the existing split-mode length decoder and preserves all previous modes. No mature codec, trained asset, special caller options, new long-distance history or second parse. The candidate is from the complete verified accepted source `8566c8a`, Git source blobs matching live docs-only `main` at qualification: root `default.pgo` `382b3c59a75bfa11bc60968f71cdde67dbe8d634`, `engine/region_fast.go` `6e6512bcfa2bf1a49a590854a11403833b96da9e`, `engine/cross.go` `5f27fde8830b9099f6bb24769f7c041968f3ce50` before disposable edits. Other production code unaffected.

**Novelty check for extra experiments:** Previous selected math group-sharing screens gave 42,162B optimistic savings. The most recent 11-component batch screened three-way opcode-conditioned lengths against *the original merged length stream*, showing 54,645B theoretical gains in a 48-block subset. This round instead compares them to the **new winning two-way length coding**, preventing a false cumulative gain claim. The other experiment avoids redundant *merged model computation* by using a lower bound from the empirical zero-order Shannon entropy. Neither swaps external codecs or replaces the existing parser.

## Other batched candidate/cost screens

1. **Three-way literal/exact/transformed length entropy**: 188 actually chosen cross-history mathematical blocks, 48 have eligible transformed references, 5/48 have positive additional gains; **only 117B of optimistic selective savings beyond the *new two-way* scheme**, and unconditionally three-way is **167B worse** across eligible blocks. **Reject this concrete three-way extra decoder subtype**: extra headers/table/training/work dominate. Do not claim 54KB earlier vs merged as an incremental gain over two-way.
2. **Entropy lower-bound early selection**: In disposable fully integrated Go source, first native-pack the two distinct length streams and conservatively skip full merged-length packing only when the split archive stream size plus **32B safety** is lower than the empirical Shannon H0 byte bound on merged lengths. On original selected Mozilla/Samba/Ooffice/Osdb, **all four candidate archive lengths and exact source reconstructions matched unoptimized type20**, 29,418,432B combined. Small ABBA run: unoptimized 6.534619s, entropy-bound 6.829617/6.694044s; a final unoptimized pass stalled and did not complete. No credible speed benefit; **do not promote this entropy-bound shortcut** or label it a validated universal size theorem without a separate rigorous framing/finite-state proof.
3. **Command-count shortcut**, based on 188 selected length-stream diagnostics, showed all 175 blocks with >=10,000 commands favored two-way splits, while only three tiny blocks lost. However, the final proposed source rework **did not execute to completion** due local working-copy permission/runtime failures. **UNTESTED**; never claim a candidate or source result, do not hardcode a corpus-derived threshold without generalization evidence.

## Decision and targeted next work

**KEEP type20 in unpromoted candidate state.** The byte savings are real and worth retaining, but a **0.134849pp** reduction with significant new wire format and inconclusive CPU timing should not be accepted on an anomalous/non-interleaved speed signal. It would be unsound to claim the new entropy-bound shortcut is faster, and gross source CPU drift makes repeating unlimited whole-corpus runs counterproductive. Credible next work is a *measurably cheaper truly byte-size-safe decision* (e.g. statistically useful coupled mode pricing with rigorous no-regression or single-pass shared histogram) that avoids the third full length encode without adding an equally expensive entropy pass, then repeat a small bounded same-host paired screen. Continue broader exclusive Zstd architectural inspiration after that; do not sweep neighboring thresholds or re-run earlier fully negative superblock variants.
