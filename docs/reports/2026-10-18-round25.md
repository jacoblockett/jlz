# Round 25: Automatically selected native byte-lane streams (accepted)

**Date:** 2026-10-18. **Base:** unchanged approved Round 23. **Decision:** **ACCEPT** after ten paired whole-corpus Silesia and ten paired heterogeneous-corpus runs, native byte-exact roundtrips, 27-input decoder compatibility audit, uncached tests, vet and targeted race tests. All permanent source changes were made directly on `main`, without a feature branch or PR.

## Hypothesis and algorithm

The historical research inventory includes exploratory *interleaved mathematical streams*, mostly tested with **Zstd as a downstream diagnostic**. That is not an accepted native JLZ codec. A distinct, small standalone representation can exploit interleaved byte fields *before* entropy coding:

1. For a 256 KiB original region, arrange positions by residue modulo an automatically chosen stride (2, 4 or 8), preserving byte order within each lane.
2. Compress each lane separately with JLZ's **existing native raw/RLE/canonical Huffman packer**. The new block type **13** carries a stride byte followed by exactly that many independently framed streams. Decoding scatters bytes back to original positions. Lane lengths are derived from the original region size, and all boundaries and trailing bytes are checked.
3. Use the existing three representative first/middle/last regions to estimate lane-wise zero-order entropy. A **single stride-eight histogram** supplies exact histograms for the smaller two and four strides by combining congruent positions, avoiding repeated scans. Only if the optimistic estimate suggests material headroom against the *already selected full archive* does the encoder serialize samples, choose the measured-best stride, and produce a candidate.
4. The candidate automatically falls back to raw blocks when appropriate. It is selected **only when its complete JLZ1 archive, including all metadata and SHA-256, is strictly smaller than the incumbent**. No user-visible modes, codec dependencies, levels, external compressor, or speculation-only settings.

This is not a claim that byte deinterleaving, Shannon entropy, or Huffman are novel algorithms. It is a verified native JLZ composition applied to original bytes, different from the historical external-codec studies. It preserves the existing independent-group decoder architecture, worker limits, and `compress`/`decompress` CLI.

## Benchmarked corpora and method

- **Full Silesia:** 12 original files, **211,938,580 B**, SHA-256 fingerprints preserved in [Round 23 corpus manifest](2026-10-18-round23-silesia-sha256.txt).
- **Heterogeneous convenience corpus:** eight locally installed source/code containers, ELF, JAR, TTC font, SQLite and SoundFont files, **70,159,124 B**. The exact per-file hashes and provenance are in [Round 24 report](2026-10-18-round24-corpus-screen.md). These are not redistributed or committed.
- **Go 1.23.2, linux/amd64**; unchanged Round 23 and candidate executables independently compiled in disposable source trees, run in alternating A/B order for **ten whole-corpus pairs on each corpus**. Per-file original input is loaded outside the encode/decode clocks; the clocks cover full native archive creation and restoration, excluding input I/O and untimed byte equality checks.
- Measurements are medians of ten **whole-corpus totals**, not sums of per-file medians. [Raw paired results](2026-10-18-round25-paired10.csv) retain exact nanoseconds for both corpora.

## Ten-pair end-to-end results

| Corpus / metric | Unchanged Round 23 | Round 25 | Change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Silesia complete archive bytes | 63,658,799 | **63,104,117** | **-554,682 B (0.87% smaller)** |
| Silesia encode median | 7.622122 s | **7.549920 s** | **0.95% faster** |
| Silesia decode median | 2.910318 s | **2.839905 s** | **2.42% faster** |
| External complete archive bytes | 32,316,619 | **32,085,884** | **-230,735 B (0.71% smaller)** |
| External encode median | **3.053475 s** | 3.076390 s | **0.75% slower** |
| External decode median | 0.773359 s | **0.739511 s** | **4.38% faster** |

**All 20 tested files retain or improve their compressed archive size.** Silesia `sao` improves **6,816,395 → 6,261,713 B**, with per-file encode median approximately **0.0693 → 0.1046 s** (~51% slower), but decode **0.0659 → 0.0324 s**. External SoundFont improves **5,766,534 → 5,535,799 B**, with encode median **0.0818 → 0.1043 s** (~27.5% slower) and decode **0.0575 → 0.0169 s**. All other Silesia and external files produce byte-identical Round 23 archives. Minor per-file timing movements on unchanged archives can reflect measurement noise; they are not asserted as algorithmic improvements.

The extra sample and lane work therefore introduces a significant encode regression on the two winning files, particularly short-encode `sao`, but aggregate Silesia encode and both corpus decode medians improve. The external encoding aggregate regression is 0.75%. Promotion considers these costs alongside measured full-archive gains and decoder speed rather than applying a fixed percentage threshold.

### Fixed reference archives, not performance baselines

Historical **Silesia** Zstd-3: **66,216,569 B**; XZ-6: **49,233,340 B**. JLZ Round 25 is **3,112,452 B smaller** than the historical Zstd-3 Silesia total and **13,870,777 B larger** than historical XZ-6. The historical Zstd/XZ CLI timings and input-byte identity are not directly comparable to these JLZ in-memory paired runs. The previously noted Round 21 historical archive discrepancy remains unresolved and is not silently reconciled.

## Validation and wire compatibility

- Native lane roundtrips for short, unaligned, fully random and 256 KiB blocks, including all allowed stride values, passed. Corrupt stride metadata, truncated metadata, and corruption of a complete lane archive are rejected. Shared-histogram results matched the independent stride histograms in numerical-equivalence tests.
- **12/12 Silesia + 8/8 external inputs** were restored byte-exactly after compression with the candidate.
- Independently created **27 Round 23 and 27 Round 25 archives** from 12 Silesia and 15 bundled fixtures. **27/27** candidate archives roundtripped. The Round 25 decoder restored **27/27** Round 23 archives. The old Round 23 decoder restored **26/27** new archives, correctly rejecting the `sao` archive that introduces block type **13**. **26/27** archives remain byte-identical. This is an explicit *forward decoder limitation*, not backward incompatibility of Round 25.
- `go test -count=1 ./...`, `go vet ./...`, and targeted `go test -race -count=1 -run 'TestLane|TestCross|TestBWTNativeRoundtrip|TestPacked' ./...` passed locally. GitHub Test is a separate release-stage verification.
- No compressed executables, sample corpora, or source files from third-party datasets were added to Git history.

## Verdict and next priority

**PROMOTE Round 25.** It improves end-to-end Silesia size, encode and decode medians and generalizes with positive archive-size/decode results beyond Silesia, at a small external aggregate encoding cost and disclosed per-file regressions. Four narrowly scoped files implement and test the new representation; existing format dispatch is extended only where required. A targeted race pattern is added to the existing cached-build CI verification.

**Next priority:** seek a larger structural gain beyond retuning three-sample thresholds. Investigate low-overhead representations for currently expensive match distance/literal payloads, while cross-checking `docs/research/attempted-ideas.md` for earlier implementations. Continue to diversify legally reusable corpora and investigate the historical Round 21 source/corpus mismatch using exact input SHA-256 and historical revision evidence. Keep all unsuccessful designs canonicalized before revisiting them.
